# fisheries-data-repo

Fisheries management areas, as data for the oceansensing.org ocean map: FAO's
Major Fishing Areas and the regional fishery bodies' areas of competence; the
U.S. Regional Fishery Management Councils' regions, NOAA Fisheries' regulated,
statistical, habitat and closed areas and the deep-sea coral protection areas;
ICES's areas and statistical rectangles; and Atlantic and Arctic Canada's
fisheries management areas, NAFO's divisions and Canada's marine refuges.
None of it is the regulation it maps, and none of it says whether an area is
open or closed today: emergency and in-season closures are never in it.

Updated daily.
